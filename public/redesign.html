Her Roots · Her Vision

The Journey of a Himalayan Trailblazer

"True empowerment is not handed down — it is built through relentless craft, financial independence, and uplifting others along the ascent."

Born and raised in the heart of Sikkim, Mrs. Shova Rai represents the modern spirit of Eastern Himalayan entrepreneurship. What began in the late 1990s as a self-taught passion for fine needlecraft, confectionery, and beauty styling soon matured into a multi-sector enterprise that transformed Gangtok’s commercial landscape.

Recognizing the lack of advanced cosmetology in the region, Shova traveled across India to train with industry masters at the premier Nalini & Yasmin Hair Academy in Mumbai and Truffle Nation in New Delhi. In 2007, she established Cutting Edge Hair & Beauty in Namnang, Gangtok — turning it into Sikkim’s landmark salon and bridal grooming studio.

Her visionary footprint expanded into sustainable fashion with Blush Boutique and organic food heritage with Zayel's Pickle (FSSAI registered, UDYAM certified), partnering with local farmers to export organic Dalle Khorsani and wild mountain preserves nationwide.
