=== RAI SHOVA WEBSITE - PHOTO UPLOAD INSTRUCTIONS ===

You can upload your photos into this `/public` folder or any folder in the AI Studio File Explorer!

1. Place your image files inside the `public/` directory, for example:
   - public/profile.jpg  (for Hero Profile photo)
   - public/about.jpg    (for About section photo)
   - public/project1.jpg (for Project 1 thumbnail)
   - public/project2.jpg (for Project 2 thumbnail)
   - public/project3.jpg (for Project 3 thumbnail)

2. On the website preview:
   - Click the "Manage Photos" button in the navigation bar OR click on any blank photo space on the page.
   - Enter the relative path (e.g., `/profile.jpg`, `/about.jpg`, `/project1.jpg`).
   - OR click "Choose File" to select an image directly from your computer!

All photo settings will be saved automatically in your browser memory.
